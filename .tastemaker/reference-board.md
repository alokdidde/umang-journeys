# Reference board

Created: 2026-08-26 · Updated: 2026-08-26
Mode: Operate inside the product; Persuade on the signed-out front door.
Design read: Guided public-service platform for citizens and evaluators. The signed-out page must explain the unfamiliar life-event model visually before asking someone to enter the demo; the authenticated product remains calm and task-first.
Dials: signed-out variance 7, motion 5, density 3, art direction 8; authenticated variance 3, motion 2, density 3, art direction 6.

## Quality bar
- GOV.UK task-list patterns: plain language, ordered steps, visible status, and a single next action.
- UMANG official portal: familiar citizen-facing identity and direct access to services.
- DigiLocker: distinct Documents and Activity destinations with provenance preserved.
- Vercel AI Elements: accessible document input and confirmation, kept subordinate until the user opens it.
- LifeSG: groups government help around life events instead of agency boundaries.
- GOV.UK One Login and task-list patterns: one trusted account, plain-language service starts, ordered work, and visible status.

## Borrow
- Palette/material: preserve UMANG blue, warm white surfaces, and restrained purple AI emphasis.
- Type/hierarchy: large plain-language screen titles and comfortable 15–17px explanatory copy.
- Layout/composition: one reading lane and one next action inside the app; a short problem-to-solution narrative on the public front door.
- Motion/interaction: short state feedback in the app; restrained GSAP scroll storytelling on the signed-out page only, with a complete reduced-motion path.
- Asset language: status icons, document previews, and journey glyphs that carry meaning.
- Health journeys: surface policy limits and verification gaps as decisions, and keep eligibility, identity, and cashless approval explicitly outside the sandbox result.

## Avoid
- Permanent side navigation, KPI strips, two-column workbenches, and equal-weight information walls.
- Technical service metadata in the main task flow.
- Multiple active journey cards competing on Home.
- A full-screen chatbot or an assistant that hides the document library.
- Small operational labels used to manufacture density.

## Direction contract
- Thesis: UMANG should tell the citizen the one useful thing to do next, then get out of the way.
- First viewport: a plain promise, one primary demo action, and a real branching journey preview; no feature grid or abstract slogans.
- System: locked UMANG palette, familiar top navigation, real workflow fragments, progressive disclosure inside the app, and restrained explanatory motion on the public page.
- Risk: turning the public page into a dense feature inventory, or carrying marketing-scale type and spacing into authenticated screens.

## Signed-out asset cast
- Hero anchor: constructed “Having a baby” journey fragment using real product statuses and branch semantics.
- Mode range: 6 real life-event journey glyphs from the existing Lucide family.
- Process artifacts: natural-language intake, beneficiary confirmation, next-task card, and vehicle-registration evidence match.
- Proof: the implemented 6 journey families, document/activity persistence, and explicit synthetic-service boundary.
- Texture object: `public/assets/journey-landscape.png`, framing the opening and demo-access close.
- Micro assets: existing UMANG mark, Lucide journey/status icons, connectors, and numbered story markers.
- Rejected: stock civic photography, invented testimonials, fake browser chrome, KPI strips, and generic feature-card grids.
